ADR-002: Risk Calculation Methods

== Status
Accepted

== Context
Financial risk management uses sophisticated mathematical methods that are:
* Complex for non-financial professionals
* Computationally intensive
* Originally designed for trading portfolios
* Need adaptation for product development context

== Decision
We will implement simplified but mathematically sound versions of:
* *Value at Risk (VaR)* using Monte Carlo simulation
* *Conditional Value at Risk (CVaR)* for portfolio-level sprint risk
* *Bootstrap sampling* for confidence intervals
* *Scenario-based stress testing* for extreme cases

All methods will be:
* Explained in business terms
* Visualized clearly
* Limited to essential parameters
* Validated against known benchmarks

== Consequences

=== Positive
* Brings proven risk methods to product development
* Provides quantitative basis for decisions
* Enables portfolio-level sprint planning
* Creates common risk language across teams

=== Negative
* Simplification may reduce accuracy
* Requires education for proper interpretation
* Risk of false precision in estimates

=== Mitigation
* Clear documentation of limitations
* Multiple scenario analysis
* Regular validation against actual outcomes
* Progressive disclosure of complexity
